### Cardiovascular Health Guidelines
- To reduce heart disease risk, maintain a diet low in sodium and saturated fats.
- Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity or 75 minutes of vigorous activity per week.
- Smoking cessation is critical for lowering the immediate risk of heart attack and stroke.
- Monitor your blood pressure regularly; the target for most adults is below 120/80 mmHg.
- Manage stress through meditation, deep breathing, or physical activity.
- Limit alcohol consumption to moderate levels (1 drink per day for women, 2 for men).

### Diabetes Management Guidelines
- Focus on low-glycemic index foods like whole grains, legumes, and non-starchy vegetables.
- Regular physical activity help improve insulin sensitivity and manage blood sugar levels.
- Maintain a healthy body weight through portion control and balanced nutrition.
- Avoid sugary beverages and processed snacks to prevent blood glucose spikes.
- Monitor blood sugar levels as recommended by your healthcare provider.

### Hypertension (High Blood Pressure) Guidelines
- Follow the DASH (Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension) diet, which emphasizes fruits, vegetables, and low-fat dairy.
- Reduce daily sodium intake to less than 2,300 mg, ideally under 1,500 mg for those with hypertension.
- Potassium-rich foods like bananas, spinach, and sweet potatoes can help lower blood pressure.
- Regular aerobic exercises like brisk walking, swimming, or cycling are highly effective.

### General Wellness and Lifestyle
- Prioritize 7-9 hours of quality sleep each night for metabolic and mental health.
- Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water throughout the day.
- Regular check-ups with a healthcare professional are essential for early detection of health issues.
- Maintain social connections and engage in hobbies to support emotional well-being.
